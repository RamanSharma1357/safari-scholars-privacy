1. Introduction

Welcome to Safari Scholars ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Safari Scholars. Safari Scholars is an educational learning game intended to provide an engaging and child-friendly learning experience.

2. Information We Collect

Safari Scholars does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, address, phone number, or password.

We do not intentionally collect, sell, rent, or share personal information from users through the App.

3. Children's Privacy

Safari Scholars is designed as an educational application and may be used by children. We take children's privacy seriously.

The App does not intentionally collect personal information from children. We encourage parents or guardians to supervise children's use of mobile applications and to contact us if they believe that personal information has been provided to us.

4. Data Storage

Safari Scholars does not maintain a user account system or a remote database for storing personal user information.

Any information that may be generated locally by the App, such as gameplay state or scores, is intended to remain on the user's device unless the App explicitly provides a sharing or transmission feature.

5. Third-Party Services and External Resources

The App may use third-party technical resources, such as web fonts, to display its interface correctly. For example, if Google Fonts are included in the version of the App you use, your device may connect to Google's servers to retrieve those font resources.

Third-party services may process technical information such as an IP address as part of delivering their resources. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

We do not use the App to intentionally collect personal information for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

6. Advertising and Tracking

Safari Scholars is not intended to use personal-data-based advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not intentionally create user profiles for advertising purposes.

7. Data Security

Because Safari Scholars does not intentionally collect or maintain personal information, there is no personal-user database maintained by the App developer. We nevertheless take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection or disclosure of user information.

8. Links to Other Websites

The App may, in future versions, contain links to external websites. If you follow an external link, you will leave Safari Scholars and the external website's privacy policy will apply. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.
